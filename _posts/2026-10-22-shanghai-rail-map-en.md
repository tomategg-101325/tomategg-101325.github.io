Due to formatting limitations, the official railway map of Shanghai Metro has room for visualization enhancements. Therefore I made a map myself, also taking into account railways owned by the China Railway Company and Songjiang Tram.

Tool used: [aarc.jowei19.com](https://aarc.jowei19.com)

**I created this image myself. Please credit the source when reposting!**

2026.10.24 v1.3 version. Click [here](/assets/images/shanghai-rail/shanghai-rail-map-20261024.svg) for the svg file。

- Removed some features that are not planned in the near future
